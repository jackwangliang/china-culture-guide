# 08 — Accounts, Payments and the App Map

Use this reference when a foreigner is stuck before they can even start: download, registration, SMS, identity verification, or payment. Diagnose **which of the four gates is failing** before giving steps.

Everything operational here is version- and policy-dependent. State "verify in-app / on the official site" and see `10-accuracy-sources-and-scope.md`.

| Gate | Typical symptom |
|---|---|
| 1. Download | App not in the local store, or a different edition is installed |
| 2. Registration | SMS code never arrives, or the number is rejected |
| 3. Identity / features | Wallet, Channels or group features missing |
| 4. Payment | Card declines, 服务 / Wallet absent, "payment failed" |

## 1. Download and names

- Depending on store region, the messaging app may appear as **WeChat** or **Weixin (微信)**. The services interconnect, but feature availability (Wallet, Channels, some Mini Programs) can differ by account region. Do not claim the two are identical products.
- Other apps: **QQ**, **Alipay (支付宝)**, **Xiaohongshu / RedNote (小红书)**, **Bilibili (哔哩哔哩 / B站)**, **Douyin (抖音)**, **Weibo (微博)**, **Zhihu (知乎)**, plus travel tools such as Amap / Baidu Maps, 12306 for trains, Didi, Meituan and Taobao.
- Install only from the official app store or the publisher's official site. Avoid third-party APK mirrors and "international unlock" tools.

## 2. Registering with a foreign phone number

1. Install or update the app, choose Sign up / 注册 (zhùcè), enter a mobile number that can receive SMS.
2. Official Weixin Pay guidance states a mainland (+86) number is **not** required — an international number works as long as it can receive verification codes. Availability still depends on the current app version and region.
3. If the SMS does not arrive: check the country code, retry once after a few minutes, try the voice-verification option if offered, then use the in-app help centre. Do not use a stranger's number or a paid "verification service".
4. Some regions offer an English interface; otherwise use the UI glossary below. Setting the display language early makes the rest much easier.
5. Never share an SMS code, passport photo or login with anyone who offers to "help verify" your account.

## 3. Setting up payment with an international card (Weixin Pay)

Official path from the WeChat Help Centre and Weixin Pay documentation:

1. **Me / 我** → **Pay and Services / 服务** (fúwù) → **Wallet / 钱包** (qiánbāo).
2. **Bank Cards / 银行卡** → **Add a Bank Card / 添加银行卡**.
3. Accept the service agreement and privacy policy, fill in identity information, and add the card. A passport may be requested for identity verification; a small pre-authorisation charge can appear temporarily.
4. Complete any additional checks shown on screen. Ensure the name matches the card exactly.
5. Pay by scanning a merchant QR code (扫一扫 / sǎo yī sǎo) or showing the in-app payment code.

**What official documentation says:**

- A mainland phone number is not inherently required; a passport or other eligible document is used for identity verification.
- Supported international networks have included Visa, Mastercard, American Express, JCB, Diners Club / Discover and UnionPay international. Eligibility is still account- and issuer-specific.
- The international-card route is designed for **everyday merchant purchases in mainland China**. Official visitor guidance says it does not support transfers to individuals or red packets; some accounts that complete additional real-name verification may see balance features. Follow your own app's screens rather than a third-party claim.
- Fees and limits have been revised more than once. A commonly documented rule is no service fee on single transactions of RMB 200 or less and a 3% fee above that, with transaction and annual caps. **Treat all figures as illustrative and verify on the final payment screen or in the official help centre.**
- Some personal merchant QR codes do not accept international-card-funded payments. Set up **Alipay with an international card as a backup** before travelling; travellers commonly find merchant acceptance differs between the two.
- Neither service requires a Chinese bank account for the basic visitor setup.

**If 服务 / Wallet is missing:** official guidance suggests **Me → Settings / 设置 → General / 通用 → Tools or Accessibility / 辅助功能 → enable WeChat Pay / 微信支付**, or an in-app scan-and-enable route. Menus vary by version. Never create a new account or use another person's identity just to expose the menu.

## 4. Entry policy and travel facts

Two separate common schemes are often confused: unilateral visa-free entry (up to 30 days for eligible nationalities) and the 240-hour (10-day) transit visa-free scheme, which was extended to 57 countries in August 2026 and requires an onward ticket to a third country or region.

- Do **not** memorise or reproduce a country list here — eligibility changes frequently.
- Verify at the National Immigration Administration (nia.gov.cn), the Chinese embassy/consulate site for the traveller's country, or the 24-hour 12367 service hotline (+86-12367, English available).
- This project gives no definitive visa advice. Work, study and journalism require the appropriate visa in advance.

## Troubleshooting quick table

| Problem | First thing to try |
|---|---|
| SMS code missing | Check country code, wait a few minutes, use voice verification, contact in-app support |
| No 服务 / Wallet | Update the app; try 设置 → 通用 → 辅助功能 → 微信支付; check account region |
| Card declined | Confirm the issuer permits wallet linking; check name match; try Alipay as backup |
| "Payment failed" at a small shop | The merchant may use a personal QR code that rejects international cards; try the other payment app |
| App store shows a different app name | Check whether it is WeChat or Weixin; confirm the publisher before installing |
| Group features missing | Check whether identity verification is required in-app; use only the official flow |
| English interface gone | Look under 设置 → 通用 → 多语言 / Language; availability varies |

## Privacy

Never upload passport photos outside the official in-app verification flow. Never share verification codes, full card numbers, home addresses or account passwords with online acquaintances, group admins or "support agents" in DMs. See `10-accuracy-sources-and-scope.md`.
