# 09 — Bilingual Phrasebook and Message Templates

Copy directly from here. Every entry has Chinese characters, pinyin and a plain-English meaning. Pinyin uses dictionary tones; in natural speech tones change slightly (tone sandhi), so do not worry if a speaker sounds different. Replace bracketed parts before sending.

Use these as **templates, not scripts to mass-send**. A message that clearly refers to one specific person or post gets far better replies than a generic one.

## Greetings and basics

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 你好 / 您好 | nǐ hǎo / nín hǎo | Hello / Hello (polite) |
| 谢谢 | xièxie | Thank you |
| 不客气 | bù kèqi | You're welcome |
| 不好意思 | bù hǎoyìsi | Sorry / excuse me |
| 没关系 | méi guānxi | It's fine, no problem |
| 请问… | qǐngwèn… | Excuse me, may I ask… |
| 很高兴认识你 | hěn gāoxìng rènshi nǐ | Nice to meet you |
| 吃了吗 | chī le ma | "Have you eaten?" (a greeting, not an invitation) |
| 吃了 | chī le | "I've eaten" (a normal reply) |

## First contact and adding someone

> 你好！我看到你关于 [茶] 的帖子，很有意思。我也刚开始学 [茶道]。能推荐一些入门内容吗？谢谢！
> Nǐ hǎo! Wǒ kàn dào nǐ guānyú [chá] de tiězi, hěn yǒu yìsi. Wǒ yě gāng kāishǐ xué [chádào]. Néng tuījiàn yìxiē rùmén nèiróng ma? Xièxie!
> "Hi! I saw your post about [tea], it was really interesting. I've just started learning about [the tea ceremony]. Could you recommend something for beginners? Thanks!"

## QQ group verification message

> 你好！我来自 [巴西]，正在学中文，对 [历史] 很感兴趣。希望加入交流学习，我会遵守群规，谢谢！
> Nǐ hǎo! Wǒ láizì [Bāxī], zhèngzài xué Zhōngwén, duì [lìshǐ] hěn gǎn xìngqù. Xīwàng jiārù jiāoliú xuéxí, wǒ huì zūnshǒu qúnguī, xièxie!
> "Hi! I'm from [Brazil], learning Chinese and interested in [history]. I'd like to join and exchange ideas. I'll follow the group rules. Thanks!"

Short alternative: 想加入学习交流，谢谢！/ xiǎng jiārù xuéxí jiāoliú, xièxie! = "I'd like to join to learn and exchange. Thanks!"

## Commenting on a post (RedNote / Bilibili / Channels)

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 学到了，谢谢分享！ | xué dào le, xièxie fēnxiǎng! | Learned something, thanks for sharing! |
| 请问这个叫什么？ | qǐngwèn zhè ge jiào shénme? | What is this called? |
| 我是外国朋友，刚开始学中文。 | wǒ shì wàiguó péngyou, gāng kāishǐ xué Zhōngwén. | I'm a foreign friend just starting to learn Chinese. |
| 可以问一下做法吗？ | kěyǐ wèn yíxià zuòfǎ ma? | May I ask how it's made? |

Keep the first comment about the content, not about yourself.

## Asking about a custom (very effective with new friends)

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 你们家会这样吗？ | nǐmen jiā huì zhèyàng ma? | Does your family do it this way? |
| 这个习俗有什么来历？ | zhè ge xísú yǒu shénme láilì? | What's the story behind this custom? |
| 为什么要把「福」字倒着贴？ | wèi shénme yào bǎ "fú" zì dào zhe tiē? | Why is the character 福 hung upside down? |
| 年轻人也这样吗？ | niánqīngrén yě zhèyàng ma? | Do young people do this too? |

Asking "does your family…?" avoids the trap of assuming one rule for everyone.

## Boundaries and safety

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我不太方便分享个人信息。 | wǒ bù tài fāngbiàn fēnxiǎng gèrén xìnxī. | I'd rather not share personal information. |
| 请别再发这个了。 | qǐng bié zài fā zhè ge le. | Please stop sending this. |
| 我不聊这个话题，可以聊聊 [美食] 吗？ | wǒ bù liáo zhè ge huàtí, kěyǐ liáoliao [měishí] ma? | I'd rather not discuss that — could we talk about [food] instead? |
| 我需要帮助。 | wǒ xūyào bāngzhù. | I need help. |

The third line is a calm, polite way to keep a conversation in the friendly, everyday zone.

## Usage notes for the assistant

- Give **one** template per answer, adapted to the user's real country and interest; do not dump the whole page.
- Warn that machine translation of a long paragraph often sounds unnatural; short lines work better.
- Remind beginners that a small language mistake is fine — 不好意思 plus a smile resolves almost all of them.
