# Discovery menu — where to start

This file is an **entry menu, not a knowledge base**. It answers "what should I explore?" and "where do I find people posting about it?". For the history or meaning of any item on this list, use `framework.md` + `anchors.md` + `evidence.md`. Do not grow this file into ten encyclopaedia chapters.

Legacy filename retained so existing links keep working.

## Where the ranking comes from

A 2025 CCTV / Renmin University global survey of overseas respondents reported interest in Chinese cultural elements: food 78.4%, traditional medicine 70.2%, festivals 67.5%, traditional arts 67.1%, martial arts 64.2%, film/TV 63.7%, the Chinese language 60.1%, literature 56.9%, music 47.3%; 77.2% followed Chinese technology or its applications. Inbound-travel reporting adds recognition figures such as the Great Wall 42.98% and pandas 34.72%, plus official reporting of over 20 million visa-free entries in 2024 as inbound travel expanded. Two social signals matter too: the January 2025 RedNote / Xiaohongshu migration, and the 2025–26 "Becoming Chinese" interest in everyday habits.

Numbers describe interest in **different surveys with different populations and dates**, not importance. Order here also reflects how easily a beginner can act today. Sources and the full citation policy are in `10-accuracy-sources-and-scope.md`.

## Finding creators: a method, never a made-up list

1. Turn the hobby into a 2–4 character Chinese noun; do not machine-translate a whole sentence.
2. Add an intent suffix: 入门 / 推荐 / 教程 / 科普 / 测评 / 合集 (full table in `05-wechat-search-and-accounts.md`).
3. Search on two platforms — one text/photo, one video — and open 5–10 results.
4. Judge quality by 原创 (original posts), steady cadence, real comments, a visible 认证 line where shown, and the ratio of explanation to product selling.
5. Follow two, not twenty. Star one 公众号 and pin one group.
6. Train the feed with 收藏 / 点赞 / 不感兴趣; review after a week.
7. Once a week search a topic outside the usual interests to avoid a narrow bubble.

**Never output invented creator names, handles, group numbers or article links.** Teach the search string and the evaluation criteria instead, so the answer stays correct as platforms change.

## The 15-minute first session

1. Pick one theme overlapping a real hobby (2 min).
2. Build one Chinese keyword plus an intent suffix (2 min).
3. Search it in 搜一搜 → 文章 and on one visual platform (5 min).
4. Save three posts, follow two accounts (3 min).
5. Write one specific comment or one new vocabulary note (3 min).

End by asking which saved post the reader liked and why. The next session should follow that answer, not this ranking.

## When a reader asks what a theme *means*

Stop using this menu. "Why do people share dishes?", "when did stir-frying begin?", "is 汉服 really ancient?" are cultural-domain questions — switch to the four-step workflow in `framework.md`. `cases/stir-fry.md` shows a full example of exactly that switch.
