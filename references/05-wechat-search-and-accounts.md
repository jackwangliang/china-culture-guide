# 05 — WeChat Search, Official Account Articles and Finding Creators

Use this reference for "how do I find articles on WeChat", 搜一搜, 公众号 (Official Accounts), 视频号 (Channels), following creators by interest, and reading Chinese articles with limited Chinese. All menu names below are Chinese UI labels with pinyin and English gloss; **labels and entry points differ by WeChat version, OS and account region — always follow the on-screen wording and verify in-app.**

## What 搜一搜 is

微信搜一搜 / Wēixìn sōu yī sōu ("WeChat Search" or "Search") is WeChat's own search engine. It searches Official Account articles, accounts, Channels video, Mini Programs, Moments, encyclopaedia entries and web-like results — a large body of Chinese long-form writing that ordinary web search engines often do not index well. For a foreigner this is the single most valuable WeChat feature after messaging.

公众号 / gōngzhònghào ("Official Account") is a publishing account: an individual or organisation posting articles that appear in the follower's 订阅号消息 (Subscription Messages) feed. 视频号 / shìpínhào ("Channels") is WeChat's short-video side.

## Six ways into Search

| # | Entry | Steps |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Top search bar** | On the WeChat home screen, tap the magnifier / search field (placement varies: commonly top-centre on iOS, top-right on Android) and type a keyword |
| 2 | **Discover** | 发现 / fāxiàn ("Discover") → 搜一搜 / sōu yī sōu ("Search") |
| 3 | **Long-press a chat message** | Press and hold a text message → 搜一搜 → a half-screen search opens using that text as the keyword; results can be sent straight back into the chat (confirmed by Tencent's official help page) |
| 4 | **Long-press an image** | Press and hold an image → 搜一搜 → visual recognition extracts text, objects or products |
| 5 | **Voice** | In the Search screen, tap the microphone and speak a keyword |
| 6 | **Desktop WeChat** | Tap the magnifier icon on the left side of the desktop client and search articles, accounts and chat history |

Bonus: typing `#` followed by a keyword inside a chat输入框 creates a topic tag (话题 / huàtí) that links to related content.

## Reading the results page

After searching, filter with the category tabs. Typical labels (names and order change between versions):

| Tab | Pinyin | Meaning | Use it for |
|---|---|---|---|
| 综合 | zōnghé | All / Comprehensive | First look |
| 文章 | wénzhāng | Articles | **Finding long-form posts on a topic** |
| 公众号 | gōngzhònghào | Official Accounts | **Finding an account to follow** |
| 视频号 | shìpínhào | Channels | Short video creators |
| 小程序 | xiǎochéngxù | Mini Programs | Tools and services |
| 朋友圈 / 新闻 / 百科 | péngyouquān / xīnwén / bǎikē | Moments / News / Encyclopaedia | Context and quick facts |

Newer versions add optional features such as 搜索指定内容 ("search specified content", choose which categories to include via 页面设置 / Page Settings), 大家在搜 ("trending searches") and an AI 搜索 entry. These are rolled out gradually and may not appear on every account. Treat them as optional, never as required steps.

## Searching inside one specific account

Useful when an article was read months ago and only the topic is remembered:

1. 通讯录 / tōngxùnlù ("Contacts") → 公众号 / gōngzhònghào ("Official Accounts") → tap the account name.
2. Tap the profile/person icon at the top right → the account home page → tap its search icon → enter a keyword. Only that account's history is searched.
3. Alternative: from any article by that account, tap the blue account name under the title to open its home page, then search.
4. Some accounts run an automated assistant: send a keyword into the account's message box and it replies with related articles.

## Building Chinese keywords (the skill that actually unlocks results)

Chinese search works best with 2–6 character noun phrases plus an intent word. English keywords return far fewer results.

**Formula:** `topic noun` + `intent suffix` + optional `year`

| Intent suffix | Pinyin | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 入门 | rùmén | beginner's introduction |
| 推荐 | tuījiàn | recommendations |
| 教程 | jiàochéng | how-to / tutorial |
| 攻略 | gōnglüè | practical guide (travel, events) |
| 科普 | kēpǔ | popular-science explainer |
| 测评 | cèpíng | hands-on review |
| 合集 | héjí | collection / roundup |
| 新手 | xīnshǒu | for beginners |

**Worked examples** (keywords only — not account names):

| Interest | Search these |
|---|---|
| Tea | 茶道 入门 / 功夫茶 教程 / 茶叶 推荐 |
| Hanfu & traditional dress | 汉服 入门 / 国风 穿搭 / 传统服饰 科普 |
| Photography | 街拍 技巧 / 摄影 入门 / 相机 测评 |
| Hot pot & home cooking | 火锅 攻略 / 家常菜 教程 / 川菜 入门 |
| Chinese history | 朝代 科普 / 文物 合集 / 博物馆 攻略 |
| Mandarin learning | 拼音 教程 / HSK 备考 / 学中文 方法 |
| Tai Chi / wellness | 八段锦 教程 / 太极 入门 / 节气 养生 |

**Try synonyms when results are thin:** 汉服 ↔ 国风 ↔ 传统服饰; 学中文 ↔ 汉语学习 ↔ 对外汉语. Search both Chinese and English once, then continue in Chinese. Add the current year for anything time-sensitive, and cross-check facts against an official source (`10-accuracy-sources-and-scope.md`).

## Following accounts and creators by interest

1. Search the keyword → open the 公众号 or 视频号 tab.
2. Open a few candidate accounts and check: posting frequency, 原创 (yuánchuàng, original content), whether most posts are reshares or pure product sales, whether comments are real, and the verified 认证 (rènzhèng) information and its 认证主体 (verifying entity) where shown.
3. Tap 关注 / guānzhù ("Follow") or 订阅 / dìngyuè ("Subscribe"). For Official Accounts, new articles land in 订阅号消息 / dìngyuèhào xiāoxī ("Subscription Messages").
4. Use 星标 / xīngbiāo ("Star") for a few accounts so their posts are not buried, and 置顶 / zhìdǐng ("Pin") for one or two.
5. **Train the feed**: 点赞 (like), 在看 ("Wow"/share to friends), 收藏 (favourite), 分享 (share), and long-press → 不感兴趣 ("Not interested") on off-topic recommendations. Channels and Xiaohongshu feeds react strongly to saves and watch time; search history also shapes suggestions.
6. **Rebalance weekly**: unfollow accounts that are all advertising, and deliberately search one new keyword outside your usual interests to avoid a narrow bubble.

Never invent specific account names, article titles or handles in an answer. They go stale and are often wrong; teach this search method and let the user choose from live results.

## Reading an article with limited Chinese

- Long-press text → 翻译 / fānyì ("Translate") where the built-in translation is available; coverage varies by version and region. Full-page translation may appear at the top of an article.
- Use 收藏 / shōucáng ("Favourite") to save, 浮窗 / fúchuāng ("Floating window") to keep reading later, or forward to 文件传输助手 / wénjiàn chuánshū zhùshǒu ("File Transfer Assistant", a chat with yourself) for study notes.
- Copy one paragraph into a dictionary app (see `03-learning-chinese.md`), pick three useful words, and reuse one in a comment.
- Screenshots of articles are fine for personal study; do not repost someone's writing or images publicly without permission.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to try |
|---|---|
| No 搜一搜 entry | Update WeChat; use the top search bar instead; some entry points are rolled out by region/version |
| Almost no results | Keyword too long or in English; shorten to a 2–4 character noun; add 入门/推荐; switch to the 文章 tab |
| Results are Moments, not articles | Select the 文章 category tab or enable 搜索指定内容 with only the desired categories |
| Article will not open | Check connection, update the app, or open it from the account's home page |
| Too many sales posts | Check 原创 and 认证, star two quality accounts, use 不感兴趣 on the rest |
| Cannot find 服务 / Wallet | See `08-getting-set-up.md` — this is an account/region setup issue, not a search problem |
